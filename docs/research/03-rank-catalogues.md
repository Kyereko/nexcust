# 03 — Rank catalogues

How these were read: the full texts of C.I. 76 and C.I. 92 on GhaLII were blocked. The lists below come from page-level excerpts returned by GhaLII's own search service (OCR of the gazette PDFs). "VERIFIED (excerpt)" means the rank name and its position were seen in those excerpts; a gazette copy should still be checked before go-live. Order is highest to lowest as printed.
