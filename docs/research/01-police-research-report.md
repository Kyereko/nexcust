## 4. Command structure

| Level | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head | Inspector-General of Police; "single cohesive unit ... unified command under the leadership of the IGP", subject to directions of the Police Council | police.gov.gh About | VERIFIED |

See 07-terminology-glossary.md.
