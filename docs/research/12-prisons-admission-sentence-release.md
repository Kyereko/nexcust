## 1. Admission (reception)

|---|---|---|---|

| Lawful basis — convict | "Where a person is sentenced to a term of imprisonment, the Court which sentenced him shall issu[e a warrant] of commitment ordering that the sentence shall be carried out in any prison in Ghana"; warrant is authority "to the police and prison officers to take, convey, and keep that person" (s.315(1)); Form 21 "Warrant of Commitment to Undergo Sentence Where No Alternative Punishment", addressed to the Sheriff | ACT30 s.315, Form 21 | VERIFIED |
| Lawful basis — fine defaulter | Form 24 "Warrant of Commitment on Default of Distress or of Payment" (s.319): imprisoned "for the space of [period] unless the [sum] ... be sooner paid" | ACT30 s.319, Form 24 | VERIFIED (matches the Service's "default warrants" usage, 02 §6) |

## 3. Remand rules

| Item | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult remand interval | Not more than **14 clear days** per adjournment when the accused is committed to prison (day after the adjournment counts as day 1); 30 clear days otherwise | ACT30 s.169(2) [Act 633 s.17], s.186 | VERIFIED |
| Juvenile remand | Remand warrant max **7 days**; cumulative ≤ **3 months** (≤ 6 months for capital offences); remand home, never an adult prison | JTI (Act 653 s.23) | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Juvenile trial deadline | Trial must be completed within **six months** of first appearance, otherwise absolute discharge (Act 653 s.33) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Reasonable time | Art. 14(4) release if not tried within a reasonable time (cited in headnotes, e.g. *Republic v Dodzi* [2009] GHAHC 1 summary: "Article 14(4) requires bail where trial is unreasonably delayed") | GhaLII search summary | PROVISIONAL (text of 14(4) not read) |

## 8. Comparison with NEXCUST assumptions

| NEXCUST assumption | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Admission only on a remand or committal warrant | Correct, but there are at least four warrant types (remand/commitment for trial; commitment to undergo sentence; default of payment; juvenile detention order) and a gaoler's receipt to the escort | VERIFIED |
| Transfer initiate → approve → receive | Plausible; approving authority not found | UNCONFIRMED |
| Release initiate → second officer authorises | Release is triggered by a court order, discharge warrant, end of sentence (with remission), payment, or art. 72 instrument; the committal warrant must be returned to the issuing court; Discharge Board prepares convicts | VERIFIED (legal triggers); second-officer rule is a NEXCUST control |
| System never computes release dates or remission | Consistent with law: remission is "earned" and "may be forfeited", so it is a judgement, not arithmetic. An indicative calculator is safe only if labelled and overridable | VERIFIED |
