## 1. Arrest and reception at the station

| Step / rule | What the law or source says | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| How an arrest is made | Officer "shall actually touch or confine the body of the person ... unless there be a submission to the custody by word or action" (s.3); "not ... more restraint than is necessary" (s.6) | ACT30 ss.3, 6 | VERIFIED |
| Grounds for arrest without warrant | Police may arrest without warrant a person who commits an offence in the officer's presence, obstructs police, escapes, has housebreaking implements, or has suspected stolen property (s.10(1)); or whom the officer suspects on reasonable grounds of having committed an offence, being about to commit one, being wanted on a court warrant, being a deserter, or of a foreign offence liable to extradition (s.10(2)) | ACT30 s.10 | VERIFIED |
| Informing the person | Officer "shall inform the person arrested of the cause of the arrest" and, if under a warrant, its substance (s.7). Constitution art. 14(2): a person arrested "shall be informed immediately, in a language that he understands, of the reasons for his arrest, restriction or detention and of his right to a lawyer of his choice" | ACT30 s.7; CON art. 14(2) | VERIFIED (upgrades LEG-003 from PROVISIONAL) |
| Taken to station | Arrested person "shall be taken with all reasonable de[spatch] to a police station ... and shall without delay be infor[med of the] charge against him"; while in custody must "be given reasonable facilities for obtaining le[gal advice], taking steps to furnish bail, and otherwise making arrangements for his defence or release" (s.9) | ACT30 s.9 | VERIFIED |
| Search and property | Person may be searched and articles "other than necessary wearing apparel" placed in safe custody (s.8(1)); search "with strict decency"; a woman searched only by a woman (s.8(3)); no right to examine the private person (s.8(4)); offensive weapons may always be taken (s.8(5)) | ACT30 s.8 | VERIFIED |
| Refusal to give name | May be held to ascertain name/residence; if not ascertained "within twenty-four hours" or no bond given, must be brought before the nearest court (s.11(4)) | ACT30 s.11 | VERIFIED |
| Arrest by private person | Must hand the person to a police officer or nearest police station "without unnecessary delay" (s.14) | ACT30 s.14 | VERIFIED |
| Fingerprints and photographs | For a person charged with an offence that "amounts [ … ] or involves fraud or dishonesty", a competent police authority "may at his discretion" take photographs, measurements, thumb and finger prints "for use and record in the Police Department" (s.108(1)); **destroyed if the person is not convicted** (s.108(2)) | ACT30 s.108 | VERIFIED |
| Forms to CDSB | Stations send fingerprints and crime details on CID Forms 6 and 8 | police.gov.gh CDSB (08 GPS-CID-003) | VERIFIED (prior run) |
| Mental health | "A person arrested for a criminal act and in police custody shall be assessed ... by a mental health practitioner within forty-eight hours if there is suspicion" of mental disorder | A846 s.76 | VERIFIED (excerpt) |
| Informing relatives | No Ghanaian rule found requiring police to notify relatives of an adult (s.9 gives the detainee "facilities" to arrange defence/release) | — | UNCONFIRMED |
| Charge office books (station diary, cells register, property/exhibit register) | Not found in any official source this session. ## 2. The 48-hour rule and police enquiry bail

| Item | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitutional limit | Person arrested/detained and not released "shall be brought before a court within forty-eight hours after the arrest, restriction or detention" | CON art. 14(3) (08 LEG-001) | VERIFIED |
| Statutory limit | "A person taken into custody without a warrant in connection with any offence shall be released fr[om custody] not later than **forty-eight hours after his arrest** unless he is earlier brought before a court of competent [jurisdiction]" [as amended by Act 633 of 2002] | ACT30 s.15(1) | VERIFIED |
| Weekends and holidays | 48 hours "includes weekends and public holidays" | Kpedu v A-G [2019] GHASC 90 (08 LEG-002) | VERIFIED |
| Police enquiry bail — legal basis | "A person so taken into custody may at any time whether before or after the expiration of the sai[d 48 hours be] required to enter into a **bond with or without sureties for a reasonable amount** to appear before such [court or at] such police station or place and at such time as may be stated in the bond" (s.15(2)); the bond is enforceable "as if it were a bond executed by order of a court" (s.15(3)) | ACT30 s.15(2)–(3) | VERIFIED |
| Who may take the bond | A bond may be entered into "before any other Court or before any clerk of a Court, or before a [Sub-…] Police or other officer of police of equal or superior rank **or in charge of any police station**" (s.97(1)) | ACT30 s.97(1) | VERIFIED (the rank word is cut off in the copy read) |
| Cash in lieu | Court "or officer" may permit "a sum of money" to be deposited instead of a bond (s.99) | ACT30 s.99 | VERIFIED |
| High Court override | High Court or Circuit Court may direct release on bail or reduce "the bail required by a District Court or police officer" (s.96(2)) | ACT30 s.96(2) | VERIFIED |
| Monthly reporting | "Officers in charge of police station, shall report monthly to the nearest District Magistrate the cases of [persons] arrested without warrant within the limits of their respective stations and not subsequently charg[ed with an] offence, whether those persons have been admitted to bail or not" | ACT30 s.16 | VERIFIED |
| Practice | "authorities frequently detained individuals for periods longer than 48 hours without filing charges"; bail "set ... at prohibitively high levels" | USDOS 2024 | PROVISIONAL |
| Police bail form name / bond book | Not found | — | UNCONFIRMED |

## 3. Juveniles (under 18) at the police stage

| Rule | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Juvenile = person under 18 in conflict with the law (Act 653 s.1(1)) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Separate detention | Constitution art. 15(4); Act 653 gives a right to detention "separate from the area where persons other than juveniles were detained" (quoted by CHRAJ) | CON (08 LEG-006); CHRAJ-06 p.8 | VERIFIED |
| 48 hours | "any order for the detention of the juvenile offender must be made within forty-eight hours of the arrest of the juvenile" (Act 653 s.14(3)) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Privacy | No person may "release any information that may lead to the identification of the juvenile in the course of arrest, investigation or trial" (s.3(2)); offence punishable by fine or up to 12 months (s.3(3)) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Rights | Right to remain silent; to have a parent, guardian, close relative or probation officer present; to legal representation; to legal aid (s.22) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI; stated for trial) |
| Parent notification at arrest | "parents or guardians must be immediately informed of a juvenile's arrest" (attributed to the Act and UN standards, no section number) | WIKI | PROVISIONAL |
| Probation officer / social welfare at arrest | Probation officer prepares the Social Enquiry Report once charged (s.24); police-stage notification duty not found | JTI | VERIFIED (SER); police-stage duty UNCONFIRMED |
| Bail | Juvenile court may refuse bail only on the s.21(5) grounds, notwithstanding Act 30 s.96(7); bail on own undertaking or with sureties from parent/guardian/relative (s.21(3)) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Remand | Remand warrant maximum **7 days**; not extended in the juvenile's absence; cumulative remand ≤ **3 months** (≤ 6 months if the offence is punishable by death); never in an adult prison (s.23(4)–(6)) | JTI | VERIFIED (as stated by JTI) |
| Practice | Juveniles found "in adult cells" (e.g. a station in Upper West) | CHRAJ-06 p.8 | VERIFIED (as reported, 2005–2006) |

## 4. Docket flow: investigation → advice → prosecution

| Item | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mode of trial | Offences are tried summarily, on indictment, or either way (s.2); trial on indictment is by High Court or Circuit Court | ACT30 s.2 | VERIFIED |
| Who prosecutes indictable cases | "Proceedings shall not be instituted for the trial of an accused upon indictment save by or on be[half of the] Attorney-General" (s.58) | ACT30 s.58 | VERIFIED |
| Public prosecutors | Attorney-General may appoint public officers as public prosecutors "for any specified class [of] cause or matter, or for any specified area"; they appear "before any Court designated by the Attorney-General"; A-G may give written directions (s.56, as substituted by Act 633) | ACT30 s.56 | VERIFIED |
| Constitutional basis | Art. 88(3): the Attorney-General is responsible for initiation and conduct of prosecutions; art. 88(4) allows others to prosecute under the A-G's authority "in accordance with any law" (Supreme Court, *Adamtey v A-G* [2026] GHASC 60, summary: "allows statutory authorisation of prosecutors under his supervision") | CON art. 88 (GhaLII search excerpts); https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/judgment/ghasc/2026/60/eng@2026-07-29 | VERIFIED (excerpts) |
| Police Legal & Prosecution Directorate | Handles "Dockets that are earmarked for the Office of the Attorney General"; advises prosecutors on charges before court; advises on the appropriate court; its lawyers "advise and assist police prosecutors" | PLP | VERIFIED |
| Withdrawal | In District Court, prosecutor may withdraw with court consent or on A-G instructions: discharge before close of prosecution case, acquittal after (s.59) | ACT30 s.59 | VERIFIED |
| Which court levels police prosecutors appear in | Not stated on any page read (depends on A-G designation) | — | UNCONFIRMED |

## 5. From police custody to court and prison

| Item | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjournment / remand interval (adults) | Adjournment "shall not be for more than thirty clear days, or if the accused person has been c[ommitted to] prison, for more than **fourteen clear days**, the day following that on which the adjournment is made be[ing counted] as the first day" [as amended by Act 633 s.17]; applies also to committal proceedings (s.186) | ACT30 ss.169(2), 186 | VERIFIED |
| Trial on indictment | Court may adjourn and "by warrant remand the accused to some prison or other place of security"; may order production during remand; may admit to bail (s.241) | ACT30 s.241 | VERIFIED |
| Remand form | Form 14 "Warrant on Commitment for Trial or on Remand or Adjournment" addressed to police officers, commanding them to lodge the accused in a named prison "there to be imprisoned by the keeper of the said prison until" a stated date | ACT30 Second Schedule | VERIFIED |
| Practice | Detainees held "for indefinite periods by renewing warrants or simply allowing warrants to lapse"; some held "up to 11 years" | USDOS 2024 | PROVISIONAL |
| Remand in police cells | Police cells in Greater Accra held "remand prisoners" for "between 24 hours to 6 months" (2005) | CHRAJ-06 | VERIFIED (as reported, historic) |
