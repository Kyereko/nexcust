### 4.2 Applicant-visible statuses (portal)

`draft`, `submitted`, `attended`, `ready_for_review`, `under_review`, `under_investigation`, `additional_information_requested`, `approved`, `rejected`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `archived` — with messages such as "Your application is under investigation" and "Additional information is required". ### 4.3 Back-office clearance stages (portal code, `clearanceStages` chunk)

| Stage | Responsible role (portal label) | Portal description | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `attendance` | **Attendance Manager** | "Confirm that the applicant attended the clearance appointment." | VERIFIED |
| `certificate_completion` | **Logistics Manager** | "Record criminal identification, certificate location, and optional certificate details." Required: "Record criminal ident and certificate location"; finish = "Complete the application and trigger certificate delivery." | VERIFIED |
| `completed` | Logistics Manager | "Certificate updates can still be made if needed." | VERIFIED |

Other back-office features read in the bundle: "Criminal ident" radio with values **Ident / Not ident** (displayed as "Criminal ident recorded" / "No criminal ident" / "Not recorded"); upload of a "Criminal record document / certificate" (PDF or image) that requires a certificate number first; Ghana Post parcel registration with "Batch number" and "HAWB"; a work-claim model ("Claimed by", "Claim taken over"); "Open information request", "Open review flags", "Signature requests", "Pending signatories"; an **"Authorized signatures"** register with columns Title, User, Times used, Last used, Status and a stored signature image. Status: **VERIFIED (portal labels)**.

Contrast: the portal's **firearms** workflow has explicit stages "Background screening" (Background Screening Officer), "Licensing review" (Licensing Officer) and "Approval" (Approving Officer: "apply a digital signature, and issue the licence"). The **clearance** workflow in the same code has no separate review/approval stage — the records decision is recorded as the "criminal ident" result at certificate completion. **VERIFIED (portal code)**; what happens off-portal between attendance and certificate completion is UNCONFIRMED.

## 8. Comparison with NEXCUST assumptions

| NEXCUST assumption | What the evidence shows | Status of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant applies → validation → identity verification (fingerprints) → records search → review → approval by a different officer → certificate with verification code | Portal: apply → pay → book biometric appointment → attend (Attendance Manager) → [off-portal search] → certificate completion with "criminal ident" result and certificate upload (Logistics Manager) → Ghana Post delivery. Signing is by an authorised signatory for the Director-General, CID. No published "second approver" rule; no QR verification | Mixed: VERIFIED (portal), PROVISIONAL (signatory) |
| Validity 180 days | "Six months" in two press reports | PROVISIONAL |
| Disclosure "court-recorded convictions only" | Certificate wording refers to "the criminal records of the Ghana Police Service"; CDSB records described as convict records; Act 30 s.108(2) destroys prints of the non-convicted (fraud/dishonesty prosecutions). Consistent with convictions-only, but arrests/pending cases not ruled out | PROVISIONAL |
