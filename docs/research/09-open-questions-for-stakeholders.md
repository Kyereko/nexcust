# 09 — Open questions for stakeholders

Each question can be answered by a serving officer or records clerk. Answers will move items in 08-evidence-register.md from PROVISIONAL/UNCONFIRMED to VERIFIED.

## A. Ghana Police Service

1. C.I. 76 lists Regional Sergeant Major and District Sergeant Major between Inspector and Sergeant. In practice, are these ranks an officer is promoted into, or appointments held by an officer of another rank (which one)?
2. Is Cadet Officer treated as a rank for records purposes, and where does it sit relative to Chief Inspector and Assistant Superintendent?
3. What are the Service's standard written abbreviations for each rank from Chief Superintendent down to Constable, and for Deputy Inspector-General?
4. Are there any other grades we should be able to record (recruit, community police officer, special constable, general vs escort constable)?
5. Apart from C.I. 130 of 2020, has anything changed the rank structure since 2012?
6. The website lists 25 police regions. Is that list current, and can we have the official list of Divisions, Districts and Stations/Posts for each?
7. What is the difference between a Station and a Post, and does a Post report to a Station or directly to the District?
8. What is the title of the officer in charge of a station, and which roles exist on a station shift (e.g. station officer, counter NCO, crime officer, investigator)?
9. Which headquarters schedules exist today, what does "DG-PSO" stand for, and which should appear in a unit picklist?
10. Does a station CID officer report to the station/district commander, to the regional CID, or both?
11. Which books are kept at the charge office (station diary, cells book, counter book, exhibits register, bail bond book, others)? What columns does each have?
12. When a suspect is arrested, which forms are completed, in what order, and who signs each?
13. What are CID Form 6 and CID Form 8 each used for, when are they sent to CDSB, and how (dispatch, scan, upload)?
14. How is the 48 hours counted at the station — from time of arrest or time of arrival — and who is responsible for watching it?
15. Who may grant police enquiry bail, what conditions and sureties are recorded, and on what form?
16. How are case/docket numbers and station diary serial numbers constructed (prefix, station code, sequence, year)?
17. Does any station currently record occurrences or cases electronically? If so, on what system?
18. Is the Case Tracking System launched in 2018 still in use by police investigators and prosecutors? At which stations?
19. Can CDSB's Criminal Records Database or AFIS be queried from outside CID Headquarters? Is there any interface another system could use?
20. How are juveniles recorded and held at a station, and when is DOVVSU or Social Welfare brought in?
21. Who is permitted to see a suspect's record, and is every access logged today?
22. How long are station diaries, dockets and cell records kept, and who authorises disposal?

## B. Ghana Prisons Service

23. The website says 46 establishments in all 16 regions. May we have the official list with name, type and region, and does the 46 include Headquarters, the Training School or Damongo?
24. Which establishments are in the Upper West command and in Greater Accra beyond James Camp and the Senior Correctional Centre?
25. C.I. 92 mentions a "Nsawam Region" and a "Winneba Region". Do these commands still exist, or are those stations now under Eastern and Central?
26. What are the official establishment types, and what do "category B" and "category C" mean?
27. Is "Officer-in-Charge" the correct title for the head of every establishment? Who appoints an Officer-in-Charge, and is there a minimum rank per type of prison?
28. Are the rank abbreviations in C.I. 92 (DG, DDGP, DOP, DDP, ADP, C/SUPT, SUPT, DSP, ASP, SCO, CO, ACO, SGT, CPL, L/CPL, 2CO) the ones used on documents today? Are there recruit or cadet grades?
29. What documents must arrive with a person for the prison to accept them (remand warrant, warrant of commitment, others)? What is checked at the gate?
30. Which registers are written at admission, and what is the admission area and process called locally (reception, admission board)?
31. How is a prisoner number issued — per establishment, per year, or national — and does it change on transfer?
32. Which prisoner categories are used on the daily lock-up return (convict, remand, trial, condemned, lifer, debtor, juvenile, foreign national)?
33. How is remission calculated, which sentences are excluded, and how can it be lost and restored?
34. What is the correct name for a court's order to bring a prisoner to court, and which unit schedules and records court escorts?
35. How are remand warrant expiry dates tracked, and what happens when a warrant expires without a new one?
36. How are transfers between establishments authorised and recorded?
37. What steps and documents make up a discharge (end of sentence, bail, acquittal, fine paid, amnesty), and who signs?
38. What does the Criminal Records and Statistics Unit collect from stations, how often, and in what format?
39. Is the electronic case management system in use? At which stations, and what does it record?
40. Is the UNODC classification tool in use beyond the pilot prisons, and where is the result recorded?
41. How are juveniles and young offenders at the Senior Correctional Centre recorded differently from adults?

## C. Cross-institution and legal

42. When police hand a person to a prison, what document passes between the two and what reference numbers appear on it?
43. Is there any shared identifier for a person across Police, Courts and Prisons (case number, Ghana Card number, fingerprint reference)?
44. Have the Police Service and Prisons Service registered as data controllers with the Data Protection Commission, and are there internal rules for sharing prisoner or suspect data?
45. Is there any procedure for removing or sealing a criminal record after acquittal, pardon or passage of time?
46. Which non-custodial outcomes should be recordable today (fine, bond, plea agreement, community sentence)?

## D. Added 16 November 2026 (see files 10–14)

### Police clearance (CID / CDSB)

47. What is the official validity period of a police clearance certificate, and where is it stated? Press reports say six months; is that counted in calendar months from the date of issue?
48. Which office titles are registered as authorised signatories for clearance certificates, and is every certificate signed "for the Director-General, CID"? Is a second officer required to check a certificate before it is signed?
49. Between the fingerprint appointment ("attendance") and "certificate completion" on the eServices portal, which unit searches the records (CDSB, Fingerprint Bureau, AFIS), and who decides the "criminal ident" result?
50. Is "criminal ident" a fingerprint (AFIS) match, a name match, or both? How is a name-only result handled for Nominal Vetting on CID Form 196?
51. What does a certificate say when the person has a record? Does it list convictions only, or also arrests, pending cases or acquittals? Are old convictions ever left off?
52. Are fingerprints and photographs taken under Act 30 s.108 destroyed when a person is not convicted, as s.108(2) requires? Is the same done for prints taken from clearance applicants?
53. How do employers, embassies and other receiving bodies check that a certificate is genuine today, and when will the "Authentication of Police Clearance" service go online?
54. Are the "coming soon" services (board vetting for Bank of Ghana, Education, SEC; adoption vetting) handled offline today, by which unit, and at what fee?
55. Which instrument fixes the clearance fees (GHS 150 / 2,000 / 2,000 / 100 plus GHS 70), and is the US$200 quoted by the London mission the same fee?
56. Is the Ghana Card live-selfie check mandatory for every clearance type, and are applicants' biometrics retained after the certificate is issued?

### Police custody

57. What is the minimum rank (or appointment) that may grant police enquiry bail under Act 30 s.15(2) and s.97(1), and on what form is the bond recorded?
58. Is the monthly return under Act 30 s.16 (persons arrested without warrant and not charged) still sent to the District Magistrate? In what format?
59. When the 48 hours run out on a weekend or holiday, which court is used, and who at the station is responsible for watching the clock?
60. When an arrested person is suspected of mental disorder, who arranges the assessment required within 48 hours by the Mental Health Act s.76?
61. When a juvenile is arrested, who must be informed (parent/guardian, probation officer, Social Welfare), within what time, and is this written in Service Instructions?
62. Is there a written rule or practice for informing an adult detainee's family of the arrest?
63. Which dockets go to the Attorney-General's Department for advice, and at which court levels do police prosecutors appear?

### Prisons

64. May we see Ghana Prisons Service SOP Procedure 2.7 (Receiving Remand Prisoners), Procedure 5.9 (Receiving Convicts) and the SOP on Preparing Convict Prisoners for Discharge? Which registers do they require?
65. What is checked at reception besides the warrant and medical fitness (identity, search, property, photographs, fingerprints)? Is the Gaoler's Receipt (Act 30 Form 22) still issued to the police escort?
66. How is remission applied under NRCD 46 s.34 and regulation 61 of L.N. 412: who records it, who may forfeit it, and what are all the excluded categories (s.34(3)(b) and (c) are incomplete in our sources)?
67. Who computes the earliest and latest release dates, and is the calculation checked by a second officer?
68. Who has authority to order an inter-prison transfer (Officer-in-Charge, Regional Commander, Prisoner Movement Unit, Director-General)?
69. Who signs a prisoner's discharge, and is the committal warrant returned to the issuing court as Act 30 s.315(3) requires?
70. How do pardons, respites and amnesty lists under art. 72 reach each prison, and what document authorises each release?
71. Is the Case Tracking System piloted in 2019 still in use in prisons, and which establishments use it?
72. Has the Community Service Act 2026 commenced, what is its correct Act number, and will the Prisons Service have any role in supervising community service orders?

### Data protection, hosting and procurement

73. Are the Ghana Police Service and Ghana Prisons Service registered with the Data Protection Commission as data controllers, and do they rely on the s.61 crime exemption for criminal-records processing?
74. Has any police or prisons records system been designated Critical Information Infrastructure under the Cybersecurity Act or a critical database under the Electronic Transactions Act?
75. Is there a binding rule that government records must be hosted in Ghana (for example at the NITA National Data Centre)?
76. Would a no-cost demonstration on synthetic data need any procurement approval, or is a memorandum of understanding enough?
